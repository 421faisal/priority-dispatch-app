## Content Principles Applied

### ✅ Natural Language
- Replaced jargon with plain English
- Used contractions ("you're", "we're", "can't")
- Wrote like you're talking to a driver, not writing a contract

### ✅ Specific Numbers
- Changed vague "higher rates" to "$2.50–$3.50/mile"
- Added concrete examples: "under 10% deadhead", "60+ days to pay"

### ✅ Scannable Structure
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- Bullet points for lists
- Clear headings that tell what the section is about
- Icons for visual anchoring

### ✅ Benefit-Focused
- Lead with what the driver gains
- "Earn $2.80–$4.00 Per Mile" not "Premium Reefer Rates"
- "No Shady Brokers, Ever" not "Broker Vetting & Safety"

### ✅ Action Words
- "We push brokers hard" instead of "We negotiate aggressively"
- "We fight for better rates" instead of "Our dispatchers push for top rates"
- "We get you paid fast" instead of "We submit invoices immediately"

## Content Testing Checklist

Use this to validate any future page content:

- [ ] **Headline test:** Does it tell the main benefit in 5 seconds?
- [ ] **Skim test:** Can you understand the page by reading only headings?
- [ ] **Jargon test:** Would a new driver understand every word?
- [ ] **Benefit test:** Is it clear what the driver gains from every section?
- [ ] **Action test:** Are verbs active and specific? ("We push" not "We work")
- [ ] **Number test:** Are claims backed with specific rates or percentages?

## Writing Guidelines for Future Pages

When creating new content, use this voice:

**✅ DO:**
- Write like you're explaining to a friend at a truck stop
- Use "you" and "we" constantly
- Lead with numbers when you have them
- Break up long sentences with periods or dashes
- Use contractions
- Be direct about money

**❌ DON'T:**
- Use corporate buzzwords ("leverage", "synergy", "solutions")
- Write long, complex sentences
- Hide pricing or important details
- Start sentences with "In order to" or "It is important that"
- Use passive voice ("loads are booked" → "we book loads")
